## What it is
An agent skill (built for Claude-style coding agents) that produces architecture, workflow,
sequence, data-flow, and lifecycle diagrams as self-contained, animated HTML — designed to be
verifiable rather than just decorative.

## Why it might matter to us
This is the one entry here that is directly installable into our own tooling: it's a skill, the
same shape as everything under `~/.claude/skills`. Worth trialing for design-doc diagrams (we
already have `artifact-diagramming` for inline Artifact diagrams — compare the two before
adopting both, to avoid two competing diagram conventions).

## Before installing
- Read the actual skill source (not just the README) before trusting it with real project data —
  same diligence as any third-party skill.
- Check for overlap/conflict with our existing `artifact-diagramming` skill's conventions.
