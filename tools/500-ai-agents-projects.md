## What it is
A curated collection of AI agent use cases spanning healthcare, finance, education, retail, and
more, each linking out to a real open-source implementation. It's an index, not a library.

## Why it might matter to us
When scoping a new agent project (client-facing or internal), this is a fast way to check "has
someone already built something like this, and what did they use." Read it as inspiration and
prior art, not as a dependency to install.
